# Context Guard 0.17.2 — 2026-11-11

## English

### Highlights

- Context Guard automatically maintains eligible session records. Without a retention policy, sessions ended more than 30 days can move to a recoverable quarantine. Later ordinary use can permanently delete them after at least seven days there.
- Your explicit retention settings continue to apply. A disabled policy, custom period, quarantine-only setting, or durable opt-out takes precedence. A damaged or unreadable policy does not authorize deletion. A missing policy uses the automatic default, which permits permanent deletion after quarantine.
- You can upgrade `context-guard@codex-context-guard` from 0.17.1 in place. Finish tasks that still need the old Hook first, or keep them in a separate Codex environment. A 0.17.1 scheduler disable alone did not record a durable opt-out. If you want cleanup to remain off, the upgrade guide explains how to disable it before and after upgrading. [Upgrade guide](../UPGRADE_0_17_2.md)

## 简体中文

### 亮点

- Context Guard 会自动维护符合条件的会话记录。没有保留策略时，结束超过 30 天的会话可以进入可恢复隔离区。隔离至少 7 天后，后续普通使用可以触发永久删除。
- 已有的明确设置继续生效，包括已禁用的策略、自定义保留时间、仅隔离设置和持久关闭记录。策略损坏或无法读取时不会授权删除。策略缺失时使用自动默认值，允许隔离期后永久删除。
- 已通过 `context-guard@codex-context-guard` 安装 0.17.1 的用户可以原地升级。请先完成仍依赖旧 Hook 的任务，或把它们留在单独的 Codex 环境。0.17.1 仅关闭定时任务不会留下持久关闭记录。需要在升级前后保持清理关闭时，请按升级指南操作。[升级指南](../UPGRADE_0_17_2.zh-CN.md)
